Discrete mathematics concept

A road network is represented by a weighted graph G = (V, E). Vertices (V) represent intersections; edges (E) represent roads. The edge weight represents estimated travel cost and changes with congestion.

Model assumption

The demonstration allocates a 60-second cycle proportionally to the entered approach vehicle counts, with a minimum green time of 5 seconds per approach. This is an educational heuristic, not a certified traffic-signal controller.
